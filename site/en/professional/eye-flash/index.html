Eye Flash

Professional eye-contour cocktail that reduces the appearance of dark circles and puffiness while improving firmness and smoothness.

Pending regulatory review

Format
Box of 5 vials of 5 ml
Sessions
4 to 6, every 10-14 days
Maintenance
every 3-6 months
Request pricing

Professional use. Pricing is not shown.

Indicated for

  • Appearance of dark circles
  • Puffiness around the eyes
  • Fine lines around the eyes
  • Loss of firmness in the eye contour
  • Tired-looking eye area

Main actives

  • High molecular weight hyaluronic acid (1300-1500 kDa)0.25%
  • Palmitoyl tripeptide-1 and palmitoyl tetrapeptide-72%
  • Acetyl tetrapeptide-51%
  • Cynara scolymus leaf extract0.82%
  • Ginkgo biloba leaf extract1%
  • Methylsilanol mannuronate1%
  • Magnesium ascorbyl phosphate0.25%
  • Troxerutin0.05%

Clinic protocol

The cocktail is drawn into a syringe and applied drop by drop to the treatment area with the selected transdermal technique (microneedling, electroporation or ultrasound). Eye area: 1-2 ml per area depending on the indication (lower eyelid, crow's feet, upper eyelid).

Pending: clinic sequence specific to this area

Precautions

  • May contain traces of soy lecithin: exercise caution with people allergic or sensitive to soy proteins
  • Active skin infections, skin inflammation or open wounds
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Autoimmune skin diseases or severe allergies
  • Recent fillers or botulinum toxin (wait 2 weeks)
  • Mild redness or papules may appear and fade within the first 24 hours
  • Sterile single-use vial: once opened, use in the same session and discard any excess
