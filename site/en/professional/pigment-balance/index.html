Clinic protocol

The cocktail is drawn into a syringe and applied drop by drop to the treatment area with the selected transdermal technique (microneedling, electroporation or ultrasound).

  1. PrepareOsmolyte Cleanser and/or Glycilac Toner; degreasing with alcoholic solution (optional)
  2. Activate2-3 pumps of Glycolic + AHAs, wait about 5 minutes and remove with a damp towel
  3. Treatapply the selected mesococktail drop by drop with the chosen technique
  4. Boost (optional)Silk Retinol 0.3% Cream, leave on for 5 minutes and remove with a damp towel
  5. RepairHA + B5 + Silicium Mask, massage in, leave on for 10 minutes and remove any excess with water
  6. Finish and protectHA + Hibiscus Cream and Silk Proage SPF 50 Fluid

Precautions

  • Vitamin C may cause slight tingling in very reactive individuals
  • Tranexamic acid is generally well tolerated, but rare allergic reactions are possible
  • Active skin infections, skin inflammation or open wounds
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Autoimmune skin diseases or severe allergies
  • Recent fillers or botulinum toxin (wait 2 weeks)
  • Mild redness or papules may appear and fade within the first 24 hours
  • Sterile single-use vial: once opened, use in the same session and discard any excess
