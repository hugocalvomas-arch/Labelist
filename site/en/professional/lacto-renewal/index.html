Clinic protocol

Applied in layers on clean, dry skin: the first with a soaked sterile gauze (about 2 ml), the following ones with a flat brush according to tolerance. Removed with water, no neutralisation.

  1. PrepareOsmolyte Cleanser and/or Glycilac Toner; degreasing with alcoholic solution (optional, avoid on sensitive skin)
  2. Activate2-3 pumps of Pure Glycolic + AHAs, massage until absorbed
  3. Protect sensitive areas with petroleum jelly (corners of the nose, lips and around the eyes)
  4. Treatapply the selected Labelist peel in layers, waiting 3-5 minutes and observing tolerance
  5. Remove all the product with a towel moistened with water (no neutralisation required)
  6. Boost (optional)Silk Proage Retinol 0.3%, leave on for 5 minutes and remove with a damp towel
  7. Repair and finishJoy Hydrat HA+B5+Silicium Mask for 10 minutes, HA+Hibiscus cream and Silk Proage SPF50

Precautions

  • Active skin infections or open wounds
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Autoimmune skin diseases or severe allergies
  • Always assess skin type and phototype and perform an individual tolerance test before treatment
  • Do not apply on irritated, inflamed or damaged skin, or skin with active infections
  • Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes
  • Do not combine with other aggressive procedures without prior evaluation
  • In case of doubt or unexpected reaction, discontinue the treatment and consult a specialist
  • Slight peeling may occur: do not scratch, rub or manually remove the skin
  • Avoid direct sun exposure and always use sun protection during and after treatment
