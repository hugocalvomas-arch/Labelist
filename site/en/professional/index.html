The clinic protocol Pending regulatory review

The vials are sterile single doses and are used entirely in the same session. The sequence is the same for every mesococktail; peels have their own.

Mesococktails

  1. PrepareOsmolyte Cleanser and/or Glycilac Toner; degreasing with alcoholic solution (optional)
  2. Activate2-3 pumps of Glycolic + AHAs, wait about 5 minutes and remove with a damp towel
  3. Treatapply the selected mesococktail drop by drop with the chosen technique
  4. Boost (optional)Silk Retinol 0.3% Cream, leave on for 5 minutes and remove with a damp towel
  5. RepairHA + B5 + Silicium Mask, massage in, leave on for 10 minutes and remove any excess with water
  6. Finish and protectHA + Hibiscus Cream and Silk Proage SPF 50 Fluid

Peels

  1. PrepareOsmolyte Cleanser and/or Glycilac Toner; degreasing with alcoholic solution (optional, avoid on sensitive skin)
  2. Activate2-3 pumps of Pure Glycolic + AHAs, massage until absorbed
  3. Protect sensitive areas with petroleum jelly (corners of the nose, lips and around the eyes)
  4. Treatapply the selected Labelist peel in layers, waiting 3-5 minutes and observing tolerance
  5. Remove all the product with a towel moistened with water (no neutralisation required)
  6. Boost (optional)Silk Proage Retinol 0.3%, leave on for 5 minutes and remove with a damp towel
  7. Repair and finishJoy Hydrat HA+B5+Silicium Mask for 10 minutes, HA+Hibiscus cream and Silk Proage SPF50
