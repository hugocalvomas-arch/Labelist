Clinic protocol

The cocktail is drawn into a syringe and applied drop by drop to the treatment area with the selected transdermal technique (microneedling, electroporation or ultrasound). Scalp: 3-5 ml depending on the treatment area.

Pending: clinic sequence specific to this area

Precautions

  • Due to the presence of exosomes, apply superficially, point by point, in very low quantity and without leaving product deposits; mild discomfort or sensitivity may occur
  • Active scalp infections, skin inflammation or open wounds
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Autoimmune skin diseases or severe allergies
  • Known hypersensitivity to any ingredient
  • Mild redness or papules may appear and fade within the first 24 hours
  • Sterile single-use vial: once opened, use in the same session and discard any excess
