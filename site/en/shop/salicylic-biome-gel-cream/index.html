What it contains and at what concentration

  • 2%Niacinamide (vitamin B3)Balances sebum production and minimises the look of pores
  • 1%Mango extractNatural retinol-like alternative; balances the skin microbiome
  • 0.5%Salicylic acidRemoves impurities and excess sebum
  • 0.5%Panthenol (vitamin B5)Hydrates, soothes and restores the skin

How to use

After cleansing and serum, apply the gel-cream with your fingertips to four points (forehead, chin and each cheek). Massage with upward movements from the chin toward the temples, finishing on the forehead.
