What it contains and at what concentration

  • 9%Rice water extractPromotes brighter-looking skin
  • 2%Niacinamide (vitamin B3)Antioxidant, protective, brightening and anti-dark spot
  • 2%Hibiscus extractHelps improve skin elasticity and firmness

How to use

After cleansing and serum, apply the cream with your fingertips to four points (forehead, chin and each cheek). Spread with upward movements from the chin towards the temple, ending on the forehead.
