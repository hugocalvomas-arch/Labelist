What it contains and at what concentration

  • 3%Liposomal azelaic acidRefines texture and visibly reduces dark spots
  • 1.8%Tranexamic acidPrevents the appearance of new dark spots

How to use

After cleansing, apply a small amount on the face, neck and décolleté, avoiding the eye area, with gentle upward strokes until completely absorbed. Complete your routine with a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning.
