What it contains and at what concentration

  • 8%Glycolic acidMild peeling that removes dead surface cells
  • 3%Polysaccharide, rhamnose (Biosaccharide gum-2)Polysaccharide with protective and soothing properties
  • 0.25%Hyaluronic acidRetains water on the skin surface

How to use

At night, apply to clean skin, avoiding the eye area and mucous membranes; it can also be applied to the neck and décolleté. Start with 2 or 3 nights a week and, if tolerated, increase progressively.
