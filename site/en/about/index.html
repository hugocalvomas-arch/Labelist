Three ideas that shape the brand

  1. One range per skin need

    Hydration, radiance, dark spots, pro-age, shine and blemishes, and hair. Each need has its own name and colour, the same across both lines.

  2. Treated in the clinic, maintained at home

    The professional works with MedLine and recommends the homecare routine from the same range. The clinic result does not depend on another brand's product.

  3. Formulas that state what is in them

    Each product sheet states the concentration of its main actives, and five formulas have an efficacy study with instrumental measurement.
